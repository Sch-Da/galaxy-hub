# The Freiburg Galaxy team participated in CoRDI

The [Historikertag](https://www.historikertag.de/Bonn2025/) is Germany's biggest conference for historians.
Hosted every two years in a different location, it dates back to 1893 and is among Europe's largest conferences in the Humanities. 
This year, the team from Galaxy Europe joined for the first time.
Our [poster](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17016028) in the regular poster session and an introductory workshop to [Digital Humanities in Galaxy](https://digigw.hypotheses.org/6292) gave us the opportinity to reach out to historians in various fields.
